Sensor Broadcast.

Reads the sensors wired to the spare GPIOs and broadcasts the readings over LoRa. While this is on, LoRa to Serial and File Transfer are switched off. Messages keep working: they are sent in between the sensor readings.

Off: each reading is broadcast once and any number of devices can listen. On: the reading is retried up to 3 times until one device acknowledges it; use it with a single receiver.

Wiring notes.
  • Power every sensor from 3.3 V where it allows it. The GPIOs are not 5 V tolerant.
  • DHT11 / DHT22: data pin to the GPIO. Bare sensors (not on a module board) need a 10 k resistor from data to 3.3 V.
  • DS18B20: one sensor per GPIO, with a 4.7 k resistor from data to 3.3 V.
  • HC-SR04: needs 5 V, so its echo pin must go through a voltage divider (for example 1 k / 2 k) to the GPIO. The 3.3 V HC-SR04P can be wired directly.
  • Analog (LDR, soil moisture, MQ gas): the signal must stay between 0 and 3.3 V. An LDR needs a divider resistor (for example 10 k) to ground.
  • GPIO 15 is read by the chip at power-up: a sensor that holds it low at that moment only silences the boot log on the serial port, nothing else.
  • Digital on GPIO 34-39: these pins have no internal pull-up, so a switch needs an external resistor.
